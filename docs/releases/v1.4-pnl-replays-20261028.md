# v1.4 — P&L recovery, estimated replays and Genius artwork

Release tag: `v1.4-pnl-replays-20261028`. Package version remains `1.4.0`.

## User-visible behavior

- The rolling 24-hour leaderboard and KOL profile share stored totals. Exact
  results use sales less original FIFO acquisition costs and charged fees;
  transfers move inventory without creating realized income.
- Explicit `omit_unknown_costs_v1` estimates keep incomplete wallets visible
  and ranked. Known purchase components and fees remain included. Unknown
  acquisition basis and other original financial amounts remain NULL.
- Traded-token cards use the same policy and display **Estimated realized P&L**.
  The modal distinguishes realized results from a remaining position's marked
  total profit. Estimates can overstate profit until missing costs are recovered.
- Estimated trade replays retain the label in the modal and exported video.
  Missing monetary inputs do not certify exact accounting or ROI; the export
  hides ROI when the full denominator is unknown.
- Genius artwork is fetched from the exact contract's canonical public page,
  validated and converted to cached WebP. The previous worker only requested
  Flap artwork. Private-only tokens are never sent to artwork services.

## Accounting, evidence and collection

The release includes the shared settlement, metadata, discovery and historical
quote recovery fixes described in `fresh-pnl-repair-20261028.md` and
`fresh-pnl-evidence-recovery.md`. Full retained evidence and canonical identity
checks remain necessary for exact corrections. Current prices, generic decimal
defaults and provider headline P&L never establish historical purchase costs.

The estimate projection adds two nullable current-result columns:
`fresh.scope_event_results.estimated_realized_q` and
`fresh.kols.rolling_estimated_realized_q`. It introduces no snapshot tables or
periodic balance histories. A controlled projection checks original results,
positions and lots before publishing its readiness checkpoint last. Capture and
urgent evidence stages continue while derived writers wait for that marker.
Existing production projection is already complete; this release must not
reinitialize financial data or rerun the projection during deployment.

Token summary reads combine stored event-result projections and current open
lots. They do not rerun the FIFO engine, scan historical RPC state or request
provider P&L. The six-card summary batch remains bounded to the selected tokens.

Selected estimated replays may verify a monetary-only repair against retained
canonical receipt logs and a confirmed node balance. This performs bounded
current-block calls, preserves original repair diagnostics and writes no
financial data. Unknown precision, discovery or execution, conflicting balances
and missing genuine chart coverage remain blocking conditions. Portfolio
membership and public/private isolation retain their existing checks.

Modal and video share one frozen object under a signed 15-minute manifest.
An estimated lease carries its quality and policy explicitly. The browser checks
the matching accounting, market window and manifest without requiring that the
estimate pretend to be exact. Corrections, canonical changes, expiration and
private access changes invalidate authorization.
