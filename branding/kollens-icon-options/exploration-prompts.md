# Icon exploration prompts

Initial exploration: built-in imagegen, using the current rounded icon as a style reference. The subsequent user request for native SVGs without letters replaced the K concept with a lens-only design. Final SVGs are authored as geometric vector paths, with exact #F9C932 strokes and fills.

## Initial concept 1: k-lens

Create ONE new standalone logo ICON for kollens, meaning KOL + Lens. Use the supplied image only as a STYLE reference for its thick rounded strokes, flowing geometry, clean negative space and minimal construction; do not reproduce the fingerprint. Square 1024x1024 canvas, pure solid black #000000 background, icon centered with approximately 20% clear outer padding. Render as a completely flat monochrome gold mark, exact sRGB #F9C932 (RGB 249,201,50). Smooth even stroke thickness, round caps and round joins, very few shapes, crisp clean vector-like edges. Must be legible as a small header icon or avatar. No text, wordmark, letters outside the symbol, labels, extra variants, gradients, texture, metallic shading, shadows, glow, mockups, frames or other colors. The brand should convey watching influential wallets through a lens.

Direction 1 — K Lens: a simple circular camera lens housing with a small deliberate open gap at the upper-right, and a bold rounded K monogram inside. The K is made from one vertical stem and two clean diagonal arms. Keep the K distinct and readable; no line collisions with the circular rim. Make the icon feel like one cohesive custom mark, with generous breathing room between the K and lens ring. Balance technical focus and friendly rounded construction.
