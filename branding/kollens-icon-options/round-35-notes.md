# Kollens geometric chain emblems — round 35

Four original SVG constructions using repeated geometric units, uniform spacing and compact icon silhouettes. The general modular character of the [official BNB Chain symbol](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/brand-guidelines) informed the direction; its paths and layout are not used in these marks. Kollens color is #F9C932. Backgrounds are transparent.
