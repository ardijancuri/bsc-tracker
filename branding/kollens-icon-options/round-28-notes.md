## Focus Tile

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen. Vector refinement regularized edges, radii, spacing and exact color. Optic Bridge used a targeted second generation to stagger its forms. Focus Tile used two native attempts; its SVG finish retains the main tile body and removes the unwanted detached wedge.
