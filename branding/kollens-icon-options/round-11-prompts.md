Do not place a letter K inside a circle or lens, do not enclose a K monogram in a badge. No wordmark or other text, no thin strokes, no arrows, no stars, no gear teeth, no fan, no radioactive symbol, no busy repeated forks. Crisp generous negative space and elegant continuous curves. The K is revealed through the contour, not readable typographic lettering.
