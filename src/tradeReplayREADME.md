# Integrated trade replay

`KolTokenTradeModal` captures the selected KOL/token activity when **Share trade** switches its existing native dialog to the sharing view. `TradeReplayShare` renders inline content and automatically prepares and exports the video; there is no nested dialog, extra backdrop or separate generation button. The shared header retains the KOL identity and Close control. Back or Escape returns to the chart and trades, preserving filters and pagination; closing the modal leaves the KOL page. Returning or closing aborts the share session. The two-column view uses kollens's existing Manrope typography and colors, with the preview and replay controls on the left and image/video saving and sharing on the right. On mobile it becomes a single scrolling column.

The canvas drawing implementation is adapted from the user's newer `bscan-trade-replay-handoff (2)/bscan-trade-replay` folder. The supplied Downloads folder remains unchanged. The 1080 × 1350 card uses the supplied header, dotted chart, curved line, B/S circles, results panel and gold brand footer. Its text uses the app's Manrope family; all bundled weights are loaded before preview or export. Market-cap labels display MCAP. The small header brand icon and footer wordmark are synced with the app's brand artwork by `npm run brand:export`. The footer displays the native Kollens wordmark on the left and kollens.com in 30px Manrope on the right, both black for contrast on gold, with matching 49px outer margins. The UTC/pacing/gas footer line is removed.

`prepareTradeReplayData(activity, chart, position)` filters the exact wallet and token, sorts fills, preserves missing amounts, converts completed candle closes to interval-end timestamps and uses only explicitly reported supply. The replay ends at the final selected fill. `/api/kols/:address/tokens/:token/replay-chart` derives its historical window from confirmed database trades, selecting genuine GMGN 30-second, 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute or 1-hour observations according to the selected window and provider page size, then public GeckoTerminal one-minute observations from a verified token-containing pool. Provider work has a 20-second budget, coalesces identical windows, caches results and limits unique concurrent jobs. It requires completed observations bracketing all fills. Contiguous bars are preferred; sparse observations retain actual opening/closing samples and carry an explicit sparse-history flag, without fabricating empty candles or extrapolating prices. This route does not change the normal chart's available intervals.

`applyReplayMarketChart` accepts supported genuine observations covering all selected trades, preserving their actual resolution and sparse-history flag. Missing or unavailable history retains the existing chart or labelled recorded-fill fallback. The supplied renderer builds a composite curve from market observations and recorded executions, uses its readable event pacing and trailing camera, and places B/S circles above each corresponding execution vertex. Preview, seeking and export share deterministic state. Execution prices remain separate from market samples in the underlying data.

The results panel has a larger token image/name, one unlabeled moving total P&L and return row, and compact Invested / Avg. entry / Current cells. It omits individual realized/unrealized figures and their titles. The replay playhead sits around 57% of the canvas width, slightly right of center; the app's main token chart viewport is unchanged. At each revealed fill, remaining FIFO cost is recorded investment minus sale proceeds plus the backend's cumulative realized profit. Unrealized P&L values that remaining recorded quantity at the exact composite price shown at the chart tip; total P&L adds realized profit, and total return divides by revealed recorded investment. Only already revealed fills affect accounting. The sharing view explains that open amounts are estimates from recorded trade lots excluding wallet transfers and gas. A full recorded exit sets unrealized profit to zero. Complete priced quantities, full indexed coverage and matched cumulative sale accounting are required; missing valuations, oversells or incomplete history withhold estimates. Recorded-price fallback can estimate unsold recorded lots along the same monotone curve shown in the replay. The sharing view explicitly discloses that recorded trade prices, rather than observed market history, are used. Sparse-history replays likewise disclose interpolation between observations. These estimates remain distinct from independently checked wallet profit. A realized-only result is not presented as a total when open values are unavailable. The older wallet-verified gross fields still require a separately checked matching balance no more than three minutes old. No sample wallet, token or profit amount is embedded in production code.

The line, shaded gradient, glow and price guide follow the displayed total P&L in every frame: green for nonnegative profit, red for losses, and neutral gold when no total is available. The gold frame, quote badge and Kollens artwork retain their brand colors. The smaller return percentage is white, with light gray parentheses and a green/red arrow, centered vertically beside the profit amount. The three cells share label/value baselines, and their text block is centered vertically using the rendered font bounds. Below the KOL name, the badge uses only the short labels Open or Closed. Ending frames prefer the independently checked wallet balance, including when transfers make that balance differ from recorded swaps. Earlier frames and endings without a verified balance use complete, consistent recorded quantities. Closed describes the position and does not claim a profit or that every token was sold. The badge is omitted while waiting or when neither checked balance nor complete recorded quantities establishes a status; underlying verification and accounting remain unchanged.

The supplied indexed export is implemented with pinned Mediabunny 1.61.3. It encodes exactly 465 frames at 30fps over 15.5 seconds, at 8Mbps, preferring AVC MP4 and otherwise producing honestly labelled WebM when supported. Each frame has an explicit timestamp and duration. MediaRecorder is used only when WebCodecs is unavailable. Abort, encoder errors and timeouts dispose partial output. Each dialog session owns an offscreen canvas and guarded callbacks, so closing or React StrictMode cleanup cannot overwrite a reopened preview or exported file. Artwork loads through same-origin image handling; unavailable images use initials.

Save image and Copy image use the final PNG card. Save video downloads the exact prepared file. X opens an editable draft with the KOL link and caption; the saved MP4 must be attached manually because web intents cannot attach local files. Telegram shares the public KOL link. Supported devices also offer native file sharing from a fresh click. Nothing posts automatically, and cancelling native sharing preserves the prepared file.

Tests cover accounting, market-data acceptance and coverage, source pacing and geometry, indexed export and recorder cleanup, automatic-session cancellation and strict remounts, and exact-file sharing.
