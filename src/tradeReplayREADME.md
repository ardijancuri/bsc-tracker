# Integrated trade replay

Freshness checks allow up to one second of client/server clock skew for the server's balance-check time and separate current quote. Trades, inventory movements and historical observations still cannot occur after preparation time. This prevents a small device clock difference from hiding a newly verified balance while preserving stale/future-data guards.

`KolTokenTradeModal` captures the selected KOL/token activity when **Share trade** switches its existing native dialog to the sharing view. `TradeReplayShare` renders inline content and automatically prepares and exports the video; there is no nested dialog, extra backdrop or separate generation button. The shared header retains the KOL identity and Close control. Back or Escape returns to the chart and trades, preserving filters and pagination; closing the modal leaves the KOL page. Returning or closing aborts the share session. The two-column view uses kollens's existing Manrope typography and colors, with the preview and replay controls on the left and image/video saving and sharing on the right. On mobile it becomes a single scrolling column.

The canvas drawing implementation is adapted from the user's newer `bscan-trade-replay-handoff (2)/bscan-trade-replay` folder. The supplied Downloads folder remains unchanged. The 1080 × 1350 card uses the supplied header, dotted chart, curved line, B/S circles, results panel and gold brand footer. Its text uses the app's Manrope family; all bundled weights are loaded before preview or export. Market-cap labels display MCAP. The small header brand icon and footer wordmark are synced with the app's brand artwork by `npm run brand:export`. The footer displays the native Kollens wordmark on the left and kollens.com in 30px Manrope on the right, both black for contrast on gold, with matching 49px outer margins. The UTC/pacing/gas footer line is removed.

`prepareTradeReplayData(activity, chart, position)` filters the exact wallet and token, sorts fills, preserves missing amounts, converts completed candle closes to interval-end timestamps and uses only explicitly reported supply. Verified inventory carries canonical buy/sell and transfer snapshots. A closed replay ends at the later of its final fill or relevant transfer; an open replay requests `end=current` and continues through later observed market history to a separately timestamped fresh current quote. Same-block inventory balance proof supersedes older position snapshots. Every share preparation refreshes activity with `?replay=true`, without browser caching, and resumes pending verification/provider work within its bounded request budget. Missing, stale or unreconciled inventory or GMGN accounting withholds profit with a visible notice. A validated GMGN ending position freezes its own fresh price and observation time, so a later chart request cannot change the quote while retaining earlier provider profit. The current quote is not another trade and never changes recorded execution prices. Missing or stale current quotes leave the last observed ending with an explicit current-price-unavailable notice.

`server/kolTokenInventory.ts` scans both directions of token-specific ERC-20 Transfer logs from the first indexed trade through a confirmed endpoint. It checks canonical block hashes and receipts, verifies exact raw swap quantities, and deduplicates movements already represented by swaps. Newly observed swaps missing from the indexed snapshot remain pending rather than becoming transfers. Adaptive pages and receipt checkpoints resume under a six-second activity-request budget, with at most two distinct scans running. The cache lives under the configured token-chart cache directory, so the production chart-cache volume preserves it across releases. The ending `balanceOf` is read at the same endpoint block, which must itself be recent and not future-dated. Resumed scans extend to the current confirmed head before publishing fresh proof. When historical state is pruned, an opening balance can only be derived from the complete canonical movement window and that ending balance; positive opening quantities have unknown cost. Canonical standalone transfers are saved idempotently in the existing transfer table while coordinating with the worker's reorg lock. Reorgs invalidate cached evidence. Indexed trades or an empty transfer table alone never prove movement coverage.

`/api/kols/:address/tokens/:token/replay-chart` derives its historical start from confirmed database trades and its closed ending from the final swap or canonical standalone transfer; current mode uses the server's clock for its market-history endpoint. It selects genuine GMGN 30-second, 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute or 1-hour observations according to the selected window and provider page size, then public GeckoTerminal one-minute observations from a verified token-containing pool. Provider work has a 20-second budget, coalesces identical windows, caches results and limits unique concurrent jobs. Completed observations remain historical samples. A fresh forming-bar close is a separately labelled current quote, not a completed candle. Contiguous bars are preferred; sparse observations retain actual opening/closing samples and carry an explicit sparse-history flag, without fabricating empty candles or extrapolating prices. Immutable closed windows keep their longer cache; current mode uses a short cache and no browser caching. This route does not change the normal chart's available intervals.

`applyReplayMarketChart` accepts supported genuine observations covering all selected wallet movements, preserving their actual resolution and sparse-history flag. Open replays retain post-trade observations up to the fresh quote; closed ones trim them to the final movement. A current quote must have a finite positive price, be no more than three minutes old, not be in the future, and be at or after the final movement. Activity token fields and old execution prices never stand in for a current quote. Missing or unavailable history retains the existing chart or labelled recorded-fill fallback. A valid current quote can still finish that fallback; the sharing view discloses that prices between recorded trades and that quote are estimates. The supplied renderer builds a composite curve from market observations and recorded executions, uses its readable event pacing and trailing camera, and places B/S circles above each corresponding execution vertex. With a current or transfer tail, fills occupy the first 68% of multi-trade playback, the tail continues to 94%, and the final quote/profit card holds through the end. No buy/sell tooltip or marker is invented for a transfer or current endpoint. Preview, seeking and export share deterministic state. Execution prices remain separate from market samples in the underlying data.

The results panel has a larger token image/name, one unlabeled moving total P&L and return row, and compact Invested / Avg. entry / Current cells. It omits individual realized/unrealized figures and their titles. The replay playhead sits around 57% of the canvas width, slightly right of center; the app's main token chart viewport is unchanged. Replays use the separate `gmgn_total_profit_v1` accounting envelope. Outgoing transfers remove quantity and basis and credit GMGN's marked transfer value, less fees, as a transfer gain; they are never represented as sale executions. Buy fees enter lot cost, disposal fees reduce realized profit, and total return uses the fee-inclusive cost denominator. Invested displays GMGN's gross purchase cost. Unknown incoming basis or incomplete fee evidence withholds dependent estimates. Transfers affect the inventory only when their canonical movement is revealed. Unrealized P&L values the remaining quantity at the exact composite price shown at the chart tip. A fully closed position has zero unrealized profit. A separately validated provider aggregate applies only at the ending; it never changes earlier frame estimates or distributes an ending residual into earlier events. Complete movement coverage, same-block balance reconciliation, matching provider transactions and a fresh matched aggregate are required for exact ending parity. Repricing a validated final snapshot changes only remaining held value. Missing provider coverage never silently falls back to the swap-only profit ledger. The original indexed swap valuations and leaderboard accounting remain separate. No sample wallet, token or profit amount is embedded in production code.

`server/tradeReplayGmgn.ts` retrieves supported GMGN activity and top-trader position data only for requested replay preparation, using the existing server API key and shared provider rate budget. Activity queries include buys, sells and both transfer directions, with bounded pagination. Token-wide top-trader snapshots are cached and coalesced; documented profit and buy-volume sorts improve coverage without assuming the top 100 contain every wallet. The existing key cannot authorize arbitrary-wallet signed holdings, so absent aggregate positions use explicitly labelled activity estimates or unavailable results. Activity gas and DEX fields alone may omit issuer taxes: full event fees are recovered only when the trusted indexed gross/net quote and provider quote have the same proven currency. Provider aggregates remain authoritative for final totals. Persistent provider data contains public responses only; credentials stay in the server environment and never enter browser bundles.

The line, shaded gradient, glow and price guide follow the displayed total P&L in every frame: green for nonnegative profit, red for losses, and neutral gold when no total is available. The gold frame, quote badge and Kollens artwork retain their brand colors. The smaller return percentage is white, with light gray parentheses and a green/red arrow, centered vertically beside the profit amount. The three cells share label/value baselines, and their text block is centered vertically using the rendered font bounds. Below the KOL name, the badge uses only Open or Closed. Earlier frames reflect the canonical movement prefix; the ending uses the checked balance. A final transfer closes the position only when that movement is revealed. Closed describes the position and does not claim a profit or that every token was sold. The badge is omitted when neither checked balance nor verified inventory establishes a status.

The supplied indexed export is implemented with pinned Mediabunny 1.61.3. It encodes exactly 465 frames at 30fps over 15.5 seconds, at 8Mbps, preferring AVC MP4 and otherwise producing honestly labelled WebM when supported. Each frame has an explicit timestamp and duration. MediaRecorder is used only when WebCodecs is unavailable. Abort, encoder errors and timeouts dispose partial output. Each dialog session owns an offscreen canvas and guarded callbacks, so closing or React StrictMode cleanup cannot overwrite a reopened preview or exported file. Artwork loads through same-origin image handling; unavailable images use initials.

Save image and Copy image use the final PNG card. Save video downloads the exact prepared file. X opens an editable draft with the KOL link and caption; the saved MP4 must be attached manually because web intents cannot attach local files. Telegram shares the public KOL link. Supported devices also offer native file sharing from a fresh click. Nothing posts automatically, and cancelling native sharing preserves the prepared file.

Tests cover accounting, market-data acceptance and coverage, source pacing and geometry, indexed export and recorder cleanup, automatic-session cancellation and strict remounts, and exact-file sharing.
