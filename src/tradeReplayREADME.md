# Trade and token replay implementation

The server owns accounting and export eligibility. See [Node-owned financial data](../docs/node-owned-data.md) and [Durable evidence](../docs/node-evidence.md).

Public and authenticated Portfolio replay-preparation endpoints return one immutable accounting snapshot, its frozen ending valuation, an approved full-history window and genuine database market observations. Repeated polls join durable accounting work; browser preparation has one bounded poll loop and no legacy activity/chart retry chain. Closing a share modal cancels the subscriber and encoder without cancelling shared worker jobs.

Modern contracts use version 2 and truthful node, mixed or imported provenance. The exact ledger preserves FIFO costs, transfer receipt valuations and wallet-paid fees. Historical frame profit is realized gains plus the displayed remaining value minus remaining basis. ROI uses fee-inclusive acquisition capital. Unknown costs produce unavailable affected frames, rather than zero. Imported aggregate fee residuals apply only in the baseline/final state.

Long histories combine genuine coarse candles with a minute ending segment. Sparse coverage is disclosed, real timestamps stay intact, and a coarse interval cannot authorize a prolonged flat ending. Closed positions need historical market coverage, without a current quote. Open positions use the modal's frozen current mark. Token replays use the same market service and disclose estimated MCAP where applicable supply is uncertain.

The renderer keeps Kollens branding, canvas dimensions, colors, glow and deterministic seeking/export. Isolated buy/sell circles reveal a plain dollar amount for 1.35 seconds, with a short fade in and fade out. Nearby consecutive buys keep individual amounts in a chronological column: each fades in beneath its predecessor, up to four visible rows, while old rows fade and scroll upward. Burst callouts normally start at least 0.22 presentation seconds apart with a shorter lifetime. Dense ending bursts compress only their callout cadence to finish within the existing clip. The column stays together above or below its circles and can shift sideways to avoid them; unknown amounts create no rows. Source circles, raw fills, PnL, chart speed and replay duration remain unchanged. Isolated buys prefer the space above the marker and sells below; labels avoid other circles and amounts. There is no transaction tooltip box, exact-time/MCAP callout, material-sale banner, special sell zoom or size-based slowdown. Movements sharing a timestamp reveal atomically in the chart and financial state; each execution keeps its own price and identity. Transfers retain IN/OUT markers. Compact estimate/final and provenance indicators accompany the result. A separately approved ending never replaces earlier unknown costs.

Trade replays last 5–25 seconds, based on the actual approved position span and distinct movement timestamps. A bounded whole-video density floor gives busy positions more room, without inserting local pauses or stretching individual gaps. One linear clock maps source timestamps to playback from the first recorded movement/fill through the frozen ending. A 1.5-second final hold follows. The chart viewport always covers ten presentation seconds, yielding the same horizontal scrolling speed across clip lengths; price-driven vertical scaling and marker/amount fades also use elapsed presentation seconds. Durations are rounded to whole 30 fps frames. A zero-span position stays static rather than inventing history. Tight bursts can still overlap at the 25-second cap; their timestamps are never shifted to disguise that.

The pinned Mediabunny encoder reads the renderer's duration and exports 150–750 trade-replay frames at 30 fps and 1080 × 1350, including the exact final state. Token-replay duration is separate. AVC MP4 is preferred; supported WebM remains honestly labelled. MediaRecorder is only a pre-encoding capability fallback. Abort and encoding errors dispose partial output. Save image, copy image, video download and editable social share links keep their existing behavior; no automatic social posting occurs.

Regression coverage includes source identity, exact balance agreement, private membership revisions, frozen modal/video profit and ROI, moving historical profit, transfers, same-second groups, long charts, stale tails, coalescing, cancellation and actual local MP4 inspection.
